Components

Rules

CanvasFull-bleed paper. One dominant field. Avoid app cards over expressive scenes.
Chrome8px mono labels, hairline borders, pill controls. Keep controls at the edges.
DitherFour densities map to light, plane, shadow and depth. Pattern scale stays 3-7px.
MotionLong ease curves, organic phase offsets, no bounce. Motion should feel observed, not performed.
ColorMonochrome by default. If an app needs state, permit one saturated signal color under 8% of the frame.

Adoption recipe

  1. Import the four color tokens.
  2. Use paper as the app surface, never as a card tint.
  3. Choose one dither family per depth plane.
  4. Keep interface chrome outside the main visual mass.
  5. Test at 390px before adding detail.
